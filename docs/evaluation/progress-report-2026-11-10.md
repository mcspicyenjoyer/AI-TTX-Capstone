## Summary

The main achievement this period was a complete rehearsal of the implemented five-position workflow: source intake, AI extraction, explicit profile reconciliation and confirmation, package generation and review, role-specific delivery, team responses, reviewed interpretations, debrief, an immutable record snapshot and a reviewed draft after-action report (AAR). Optional reviewed images and a clearer participant/facilitator interface were added around that workflow. The result is a stronger local demonstration prototype, not final Form B completion or an independently evaluated SME product.

The remaining delivery gaps are approved response-dependent branching and recommendations, the generated ten-position workflow, independent human evaluation, human AAR acceptance and the final evidence/report/handover package. Five positions remain the authorised intermediate checkpoint; Form B's final scope has not been reduced.

## Commit review findings

- **Fixed: unsaved participant responses lost after a failed refresh (P2).** The new participant layout could unmount its response form when a same-run briefing refresh failed. The workspace now retains the current view on transient failures, but still clears private content on a run change or terminal access denial. A browser regression covers a 503, successful recovery, a 403 and a delayed refresh after denial; denied content cannot reappear from a separate display cache.
- **Corrected: cross-page terminology.** Intake uses **Document type** in both the visible and accessible label. One typed label mapping supplies the picker, imported-source list and profile register. **Package generation** now matches its page heading, and **After-action review** uses the same capitalization in its tab and headings. Current guides and assertions are updated; stored enums, source titles, quotations and exports are unchanged.
- **Fixed: ambiguous story-text parsing (P2).** The [display parser](../../src/ui/exercise-format.ts) previously promoted paragraph-start labels such as `FACT / Backup / description: test output only` or `Discussion:` quoted inside a story into application-styled sections. Because [generation](../../src/server/generation-content.ts) flattens arbitrary story text and genuine sections with identical separators, the correction renders every story-bearing flat body literally rather than guessing its boundaries. It preserves whitespace after normalizing line endings and displays the leading elapsed clock separately. Images retain their own labels without headline-based attachment guessing. The explicit tradeoff is that genuine profile facts and discussion questions within these bodies also stay plain; non-story briefing/profile formatting is retained. An adversarial regression demonstrates the fix without rewriting stored evidence, hashes, role projections or approval rules.

The independent facilitator-code review found no other actionable regression in the new package/report layout, and the subsequent parser-correction review found no actionable issue. These are assistant code reviews and bounded test evidence, not a guarantee of defect-free software. Citation navigation is tested for selected examples, not every possible record kind or keyboard-focus path.
