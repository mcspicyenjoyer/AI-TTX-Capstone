## Legal and regulatory reference

Checked 28 September 2026. Refresh these references before reusing the case at a materially different date. The fictional contract/policy terms in the register are separately attributed to SRC-REALISM.

### DPO and personal data

The PDPA accountability obligation includes designating at least one DPO and making the appropriate business contact available. Combining the function with Daphne's Finance/Admin role is a fictional staffing choice, not a requirement that Finance must be DPO. PDPC registration is not the same thing as appointment. [PDPC DPO guidance](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/data-protection/business-owner/data-protection-officers)

Do not label all customer contacts automatically subject to every PDPA obligation, or exempt an entire customer file because it is B2B. The business-contact exclusion is purpose-specific. Employment/payroll records and personal-purpose records require separate treatment. [PDPA sections 2 and 4(5)](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PDPA2012?ProvIds=pr4-)

### Notification grounds

Assess the affected fields, people, circumstances and applicable exceptions. Significant harm and significant scale are alternative grounds for notifying PDPC; the scale threshold is 500 affected individuals. Prescribed harm combinations can include identity with salary/bank-account information. Therefore a payroll breach at a 42-person firm may be notifiable without reaching 500, but merely storing HR data does not establish a breach or notification duty. [Notification Regulations, regulations 3-4 and Schedule](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/PDPA2012-S64-2021?DocDate=20241014&WholeDoc=1)

Scale alone does not require notifying individuals. For significant-harm cases, consider applicable remedial/protective-technology exceptions and directions or waivers; do not assume these also remove the PDPC notice duty. Nor is an attack on an internal server automatically the statutory exclusion for handling occurring only within an organisation. [PDPA sections 26B-26D](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PDPA2012?ProvIds=P16A-)

### Assessment and notice clocks

Begin a reasonable, expeditious assessment when there are grounds to believe a breach occurred. PDPC guidance expects completion within 30 calendar days; this is not permission to delay. Once determined notifiable, notify PDPC as soon as practicable, no later than three calendar days after the determination day. Required individual notice follows as soon as practicable, on or after PDPC notice. Keep discovery, assessment and determination timestamps distinct. [PDPC data-breach management guide, pp. 22-25](https://files.app.optical.gov.sg/pdpc/production/assets/1faa5d75-f3b2-485a-bfc4-85394c0079df.pdf)

The company profile's 12/24-hour customer clauses and insurer notice/consent terms are authored contractual context, not statutory clocks. Counsel supports the assessment; MD approval must not become a fictional excuse to miss required notification. No actual report is filed in the exercise.

### Other reporting

SingCERT can assist with cybersecurity incidents; consider a police report to SPF for suspected cybercrime. Neither replaces the PDPC assessment. Do not import CII-specific reporting deadlines into this ordinary SME. [CSA Cyber Aid](https://www.csa.gov.sg/resources/singcert/cyber-aid/)

Do not imply every internal drawing requires statutory endorsement. Clara's registration and appointment are fictional character attributes, not claims about a real registrant. [BCA structural-plan submission guidance](https://www1.bca.gov.sg/safety-and-standards/applications-and-licenses/structural-plan-submission/)

Thresholds concern gross floor area (GFA), including affected/replaced GFA for major A&A, not site area. New projects at least 30,000 sq m require CORENET X from 1 October 2025; the threshold becomes 5,000 sq m on 1 October 2026. Below 5,000 sq m remains voluntary. The larger ongoing-project exception concerns Design-Gateway-equivalent submissions, not any incidental portal filing. [BCA/URA implementation update](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/circulars/dc26-08/)
