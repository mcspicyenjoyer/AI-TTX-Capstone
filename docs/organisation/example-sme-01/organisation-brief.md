## The company

Teralqen is a civil and structural engineering consultancy founded in 2014. Its 42 employees work from an office in Ubi, from home and at customer sites. Revenue for FY2025 was approximately S$6.6 million. The firm designs and checks commercial building structures, refurbishments, and additions and alterations.

The team comprises two directors, 33 engineering and project staff, six Finance and Administration staff, and one IT Executive. Goh & Mercer Technology maintains the company's infrastructure; Beacon Nine Security provides security monitoring. Their staff are external to the 42-person team.

Office hours are 09:00-18:00 on weekdays. Site coordination and submission work regularly run later, particularly toward a delivery deadline. Two important project commitments fall before the end of this month.

## Everyday work

Each employee has a managed Windows laptop. Microsoft 365 provides company email and sign-in. Staff working away from the office use the VPN for permitted office resources; cloud email and collaboration are also used from home and customer sites.

Active CAD files and calculations are worked on in the office file service, TE-FS01. Checked issue sets and project exchanges are published to ProjectHub, the company's SharePoint libraries, with OneDrive supporting day-to-day access. Teams send links or use customer portals for large drawing and model files. Email carries correspondence, transmittals and smaller documents.

The drawing room has an A0 plotter and two A3 printers. The design team uses CAD/BIM and calculation applications, including a legacy module with a licence service in the office. Imran and Goh & Mercer keep the maintenance and exception lists.

Finance uses Finance Books, Corporate Banking and PayrollVault. Daphne's team maintains the detailed payment, personnel and privacy records separately from project working files. Project and site coordination also happens by phone and in routine WhatsApp groups.
