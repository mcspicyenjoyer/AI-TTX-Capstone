## ADR-034 - Bounded interpretation and record-only AAR at five

Verify the reviewed output and one fixed snapshot (five reviewed, zero unreviewed/stale exclusions) before an AAR call; stop on a mismatch. It does not authorise new exercise runs, profile/storyline changes, extra calls or branch implementation.

AAR implementation choice: reuse the subscription worker with an `aars` mode and the existing command receipts. SQLite v10 adds append-only AAR revisions and minimal future clarification records, without rewriting prior snapshots or exercise records. Queue/claim both enforce the three-started-attempt budget; failed, invalid, interrupted and timed-out attempts consume it. Missing usage stays null/unknown. No automatic retry or paid fallback. Explicit request/review operations identify the selected report ID/hash; later reviews or captures do not replace that source. Accept/edit/reject/manual override remain facilitator-only and do not change dispositions or run progression.

Findings are extractive, grouped as strengths/areas for improvement under the three captured objectives. Each quotation identifies a resolvable record ID and field and must match that captured field exactly. Attribution distinguishes team decisions, facilitator observations, reviewed analysis, pre-existing profile gaps, exercise-surfaced evidence gaps and origin not established. A profile-gap classification needs a full released UNKNOWN/CONFLICT provenance line; an exercise-gap classification needs an explicitly EXERCISE-SURFACED disposition line or the app's clarification-limit record. Ordinary unresolved gaps alone do not establish origin. This conservative first implementation can leave genuine pre-existing gaps with unknown origin when the snapshot does not carry their provenance. It never fetches the full profile to fill the gap. The alternative of free-form factual findings is rejected here: exact attributed extracts cannot introduce a new generated execution/recovery narrative, but quotation validity still does not prove semantic relevance, truth or a correct strength/gap classification.

AI-written improvement text is explicitly a proposal linked to improvement findings, with status fixed to proposed and owner fixed to the empty string. The user must assess recommendation quality and assign owners outside this bounded draft; no action-closure tracker is built. Human review is still required for misleading quotations or proposal wording. The original interpretation prompt, validators and corpus remain unchanged; adding the worker's AAR dispatch is not interpreter retuning or a rerun of the frozen holdout recipe.

Authorised on 27 October 2026 for frozen `package-15` and its completed three-agent trial. Implement interpretation first, then evidence-linked AAR, with tests and a local checkpoint after each verified milestone; do not push.
